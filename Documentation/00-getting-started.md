# Getting started

**Version:** 1.0.3

## Try the demo

1. Open the [live demo](https://accounting-ladger-system-coral.vercel.app), or run Demo Mode locally — see `demo-mode/README.md`.
2. On the Demo Mode login screen, choose an account card (Admin, Editor, or Viewer). Demo logins appear as account cards on that screen.
3. Follow the [Login](./01-login.md) guide for field labels and sign-in steps, then explore the [Dashboard](./02-dashboard.md) with sample Northline Advisory data.

## Production app

For the hosted production product, sign in with credentials provided by your administrator.
