## What to include in a support email

Please send:

1. The email address you use to sign in (if any)
2. Whether you are on **web** (`https://cash.trendswd.com/`), **Android** (`com.trendswd.cash`), or **Demo Mode only**
3. What you were trying to do and what went wrong
4. Approximate time of the issue (and timezone)
5. Screenshots if useful (hide sensitive ledger amounts if you prefer)

Suggested subjects:

- `Support — Accounting Ledger`
- `Bug report — Accounting Ledger`
- `Account deletion request — Accounting Ledger`
- `Privacy request — Accounting Ledger`

## Common help topics

### I cannot sign in

1. Confirm you are opening [https://cash.trendswd.com/login](https://cash.trendswd.com/login).
2. Check email/password; use the auth email flow if password reset is enabled for your deployment.
3. If you just signed up, confirm your email if the app asked you to.
4. Still stuck? Email [info@trendswd.com](mailto:info@trendswd.com) with the address you used (do not send your password).

### I only want to try the app

On `/login`, choose **Try Demo Mode**. No credentials and no production database writes. Details: [Embedded Demo Mode](./18-embedded-demo-mode.md).

### I need my data or account removed

Follow [Data & Account Deletion](./21-data-account-deletion.md), then email [info@trendswd.com](mailto:info@trendswd.com) for full account deletion.

### Android app issues

See [Android (Capacitor)](./17-android-capacitor.md). Include your device model and Android version when writing to support.

### Reviewers / portfolio

Documentation and changelog live under this `Documentation/` folder and sync to Trends WD project docs. For portfolio Demo Mode deploys, also see `demo-mode/README.md` in the repository.
